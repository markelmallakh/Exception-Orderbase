The Exception Signature

Our Chocolate Fudge Cake is built on three layers of deeply moist Belgian chocolate sponge, brushed with sugar syrup and generously filled with a velvety chocolate fudge. Every cake is finished by hand with a mirror-glaze ganache and delicate chocolate shavings.

Made fresh to order in our Cairo kitchens using the same family recipe we have perfected over the decades.

  • Silky chocolate fudge filling
  • Hand-finished ganache glaze

Frequently Asked Questions

The 1 Kg cake comfortably serves 8 to 10 people. Choose a larger size at checkout for bigger gatherings — the 1.5 Kg serves around 12 to 15 and the 2 Kg serves 16 to 20.
Every cake is baked fresh to order on the morning it ships. Keep it refrigerated and enjoy it within 3 days. For the best taste and texture, take it out of the fridge about 20 minutes before serving.
Delivery within Maadi and central Cairo is available within about 1 hour during opening hours. You can also schedule a specific date and time slot at checkout — handy for birthdays and celebrations.
